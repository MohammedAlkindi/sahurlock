Training data, per the PP-DocLayout paper (https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.17213, section 4.1): PaddlePaddle's own
set of about 30,000 document pages, among them papers, magazines, newspapers, reports, exams, handwritten notes,
contracts and books, partly from DocLayNet and PubLayNet. SahurLock's licence check rests on the Apache-2.0
grant on these weights (docs/ROADMAP.md, How the models are built).

Inputs: image [n,3,480,480] float32 and scale_factor [n,2] float32. Outputs: rows of
[class, score, x1, y1, x2, y2] in page pixels, and how many rows there are.
